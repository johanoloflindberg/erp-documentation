This page is the canonical internal catalog of services that make up the WEBBAB platform, along with their external dependencies and integration services. Each entry records the service's purpose, owner, upstream/downstream dependencies, and monitoring location. Service owners and monitoring details are marked **TODO** pending team input.

<Info>
  Keep this page up to date whenever a new service or integration is added. Open a documentation PR and assign the relevant team for review.
</Info>

## How to Read This Catalog

Each service entry follows this structure:

- **Purpose** — what the service does and why it exists.
- **Owner** — team or individual responsible (TODO where not yet assigned).
- **Dependencies** — services this entry depends on.
- **Depended on by** — services that depend on this entry.
- **Monitoring** — where to find health status and alerts.
