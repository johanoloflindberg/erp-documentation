## What CRM Integrations Do

CRM integrations typically handle the following data flows:

**WEBBAB → CRM:**
- Customer records created in WEBBAB are synced to the CRM as companies or accounts
- Contacts associated with customers are created or updated in the CRM
- Orders and projects in WEBBAB can be reflected as deals or opportunities in the CRM
- Activity records (calls, meetings, support interactions) can flow from WEBBAB to the CRM timeline

**CRM → WEBBAB:**
- New leads or prospects converted to customers in the CRM are created in WEBBAB
- Contact detail updates made in the CRM are reflected in WEBBAB
- Deal status changes in the CRM can trigger actions in WEBBAB (for example, creating a project when a deal is won)

## Source of Truth Strategy

Before configuring a CRM integration, decide which system owns which data:

| Data type | Typical source of truth | Rationale |
|-----------|------------------------|-----------|
| Customer/company record | CRM | Sales team manages account data |
| Contact details | CRM | CRM is the primary contact management tool |
| Orders and invoices | WEBBAB | Operational and financial data lives in WEBBAB |
| Projects | WEBBAB | Project delivery is managed in WEBBAB |
| Interactions and activities | CRM | Sales and CS teams log activities in the CRM |

<Warning>
  Bidirectional sync without a clear source-of-truth strategy leads to data conflicts and duplicate records. Define ownership before enabling sync in both directions.
</Warning>

## Data Mapping Considerations

CRM data models vary significantly between platforms. Common mapping challenges include:

- **Company vs. Contact** — WEBBAB customers may map to CRM Accounts, Contacts, or both. Decide on the mapping before implementation.
- **Custom fields** — Both WEBBAB and your CRM may have custom fields that need explicit mapping. Document every non-standard field.
- **Relationship models** — A WEBBAB customer may have multiple contacts in the CRM. Ensure your sync logic handles one-to-many relationships correctly.
- **Status mapping** — CRM deal stages and WEBBAB project or order statuses rarely align directly. Create an explicit status mapping table.

## Error Handling

| Error type | Action |
|------------|--------|
| Duplicate customer | Match on email or CRM ID before creating; log conflicts for manual review |
| Missing required CRM field | Log the validation error and skip the record; alert the integration owner |
| Rate limit exceeded | Back off and retry with exponential delay; most CRMs have per-minute API limits |
| Conflicting updates | Use timestamp-based conflict resolution — last-write-wins or source-of-truth wins |
